Dialoo connects you with local professionals in your city — electricians, plumbers, carpenters, AC & appliance repair, car and bike mechanics, tutors, home cleaning and more.

• Find nearby providers with ratings, experience and prices
• Book now or schedule for later — live tracking with status updates
• Get notified at every step: accepted, on the way, work started, completed
• Share a 4-digit start code so only your booked provider begins the job
• Chat or call your provider, apply coupons, rate and review
• Emergency help, equipment rentals and project requests
• Hindi and English

Are you a skilled professional? Join as a provider, get booking requests, manage jobs and grow your digital dukaan.

Dialoo verification is a trust signal, not a guarantee of service quality.
